VIBE SHIFT Support

Quick answers for purchases, saved games, player data and safe play. If the steps below do not solve it, contact the developer directly.

Version 1.2.0iPhone and iPadRequires iOS 17 or later

Quick help

Start here.

↻

Restore purchases

  1. Open VIBE SHIFT.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Choose Restore purchases.
  4. Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase.

VIBE SHIFT has one-time purchases only — no subscriptions.

✓

Purchase not showing

Check your internet connection and Apple Account, reopen the app, then try Restore purchases. If a purchase is pending, it becomes available only after Apple completes it.

For billing, refunds or payment details, contact Apple Support.

⌁

Saved game

An unfinished game is stored locally so you can continue from the home screen. Choose Discard saved game to remove it. Finishing a game also clears the saved session.

×

Delete local data

In Settings, choose Reset players to replace custom names and clear any unfinished game. Deleting the app removes all of its local data from the device.

Read the retention and deletion details.

Aa

Language or text size

Choose English, Norwegian Bokmål or Spanish in Settings. VIBE SHIFT supports iOS Dynamic Type and accessibility text sizes.

!

Report a problem

Include the app version, iOS version, device model, what you expected and what happened. A screenshot is helpful, but remove player names or other personal information first.

Safe play

Every card is optional.

Anyone can pass at any time. Respect boundaries, stop if somebody is uncomfortable, and never pressure anyone to share or do something. After Hours is intended only for adults and requires an in-app 18+ confirmation.
