Simple to start

One phone. Three steps. A whole new vibe.

No setup marathon and no rulebook. The game keeps the group moving from the first card to the final scoreboard.

Choose 2–12 players. Names stay on the device and can be reset whenever you want.

2

Choose your packs

Mix icebreakers, votes, challenges, duels and honest questions to match the group.

3

Pass the phone

Play 15, 25 or 40 rounds. Every card is optional, and anyone can always pass.
